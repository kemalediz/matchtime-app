# Does MatchTime fit a competitive Saturday league team?

**Date:** 2026-09-17
**Subject:** AFC Walcountians Vets 26/27, a Saturday 11-a-side vets league side. Kemal joined the group on 9 September as a goalkeeper and played the first game on 12 September.
**Status:** Analysis and proposal. Nothing was sent to the group, nothing was written to production, no product code was changed.
**Evidence:** the group's WhatsApp export, 9 to 17 September 2026, 203 lines, cited as `L<n>`. The transcript stays in the session scratchpad and is deliberately not in this repo. People are named only where the group's own announcements name them, and no phone numbers, surnames or bank details appear here. Code claims are cited `file:line`.

---

## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

**Partial fit. Worth one carefully scoped pilot, not a second product line.** The half of MatchTime that is commodity work (collect availability, keep a live list in the group, chase the people who have gone quiet, warn when a match is heading for a call-off, take the money) is exactly what the two managers are doing by hand every week, and most of it runs on day one with no code. The half that makes MatchTime special (two colour-balanced sides from one squad, Elo from the score, peer ratings, MoM votes) is structurally the wrong shape for a league team and should be switched off for them. So a league team buys the commodity half and none of the differentiated half, which is a real warning sign about the segment. Against that: four changes would close most of the distance, three of the four also help the existing kickabout market, and Kemal is in the group every Saturday, which is the cheapest design-partner access on offer. Recommendation in §6.

### 1.4 How the squad is chosen

Availability first, selection second, and the two are explicitly different steps:

- "That's when my fun job of balancing 20 yes' into 15 squad." (L169)
- "Going forward, with the new signings, we're going to have plenty of players and therefore have to pick a squad. ... So the squad will be picked on the basis of previous availability and commitment." (L193)
- "squad picking which me and Matt will do our best to make fair based on commitment and availability. Home games are of course popular" (L201)

Two things follow. First, **being available is not being selected**, and both managers say so in writing. Second, the stated selection criterion is a statistic nobody is currently computing: previous availability and commitment, with an explicit observation that away games attract fewer volunteers (L191).

Note the direction of travel. In the week observed the problem was the opposite, not enough players (L165, L187, L189), and anyone who offered was taken (L113 to L116, a player asking at noon on matchday and being told to come down). So the system has to handle both under-subscription and over-subscription, in consecutive weeks.

## 2. What MatchTime does today, read against that

Feature by feature, from the code.

### 2.1 Attendance and capacity

A player says IN in the group without tagging the bot, and that is enough: the interaction contract allows a player's own self-attendance untagged and requires an `@Match Time` tag for everything else (`src/lib/interaction-contract.ts:9-23`). Third-party adds are tag-free too, and an admin reporting someone else OUT is tag-free for admins (`src/lib/interaction-contract.ts:20-23`).

The write lands on a single deterministic match, the soonest active one, regardless of how full it is (`src/lib/registration-match-select.ts:63-84`, with the reasoning at `:10-21`). Capacity is then **first come, first served**:

```ts
const squadHasRoom = confirmedCount < match.maxPlayers;
const status =
  options.benchIntent === "explicit" || !squadHasRoom ? "BENCH" : "CONFIRMED";
```
`src/lib/attendance.ts:218-220`

`maxPlayers` is set once at match creation as `sport.playersPerTeam * 2` (`src/app/api/cron/generate-matches/route.ts:128`) and there is no admin screen that edits it; the only writer afterwards is a format switch (`src/app/actions/matches.ts:65`).

The instant the cap is reached the bot posts a numbered squad list and calls it locked (`src/lib/squad-announce.ts:41-51`, job queued at `:72-86`), and drops after that trigger bench offers to backfill.

Uncertainty is modelled. "Maybe, I'll confirm closer to the day" is classified as tentative, stored as a `TentativeAvailability` row, and the bot DMs that player about 24 hours before kick-off for a firm answer (`src/lib/tentative-followup.ts:1-24`, `TENTATIVE_FOLLOWUP_LEAD_MS` at `:24`, dispatch gate at `src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:465`).

Managers can do squad surgery from the dashboard: `addPlayerToMatch` with an explicit CONFIRMED or BENCH status (`src/app/actions/players.ts:718-722`), `removePlayerFromMatch` (`:834`), `moveUpFromBench` (`:882`). Every transition is written to an append-only audit table in the same transaction (`prisma/schema.prisma:1166`, rationale at `:1118-1164`).

### 2.2 Chasing, and the call-off warning

The scheduler posts on a fixed clock, all London time:

| Post | When | Where decided |
|---|---|---|
| Cold-open match announcement | 09:00 to 12:59, more than 24h out, squad still empty | `src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:778-789` |
| Daily squad update | 17:00 to 17:59, once a day | `src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:840-841` |
| Short-squad chase inside that update | same slot, when the squad is short | `src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:932-959` |
| Matchday morning nudge | 08:00 to 08:59 on the day | `src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:1391-1399` |
| Pre-kickoff chase | 3 to 4 hours out | `src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:1418-1419` |
| Last-chance plea | 0.5 to 2 hours out | `src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:1445-1446` |
| Bench slot offer, group plus DM | any time 08:00 to 21:59 while a slot is open | `src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:1189-1190` |
| Recruit chase-up DM to an invited player | 3 hours after their invite, 08:00 to 21:59 | `src/lib/recruit-chase.ts:51,64-65` |

The 17:00 update prints a numbered `Confirmed (N/M)` block plus a bench block, so every player can find their own name without scrolling (`src/lib/scheduler-copy.ts:56-78`).

And the thing that maps most directly onto L165 and L175: the update carries an explicit **at-risk of being called off** warning, computed in arithmetic rather than by the model, when kick-off is within 48 hours and the shortfall is 4 or more (`src/lib/chase-risk.ts:28`, `AT_RISK_HOURS_TO_KICKOFF` at `:39-40`, folded into the prompt at `src/lib/message-analyzer.ts:594` and `:1032`).

There is also an admin-triggered recruit blast that DMs everyone who played recently but has not answered for the upcoming match (`src/lib/recruit.ts:2-8`).

### 2.3 Polls

The bot reads poll votes, but **only on polls it posted itself**: the vote handler looks the poll up by `waMessageId` in `SentNotification` and bails with `unknown-poll` if it did not send it (`src/app/api/whatsapp/poll-vote/route.ts:38-41`). It then only acts on MoM and payment polls (`:43-47`). A poll a human created in the group is invisible to MatchTime.

### 2.4 Team balancing

MatchTime splits one squad into **two** sides. The enum has exactly two values (`prisma/schema.prisma:24-27`), the balancer refuses to run below `perTeam * 2` (`src/lib/team-balancer.ts:40-42`) and discards any surplus with `players.slice(0, perTeam * 2)` (`:58` and `:97`), and the generator repeats the same refusal (`src/lib/team-generation.ts:79-84`). The posted team sheet is two columns and nothing else (`src/lib/group-copy.ts:72-90`).

For an 11-a-side sport that means balancing needs **22 confirmed players** before it will do anything. Walcountians will never have 22.

### 2.5 Ratings, MoM and scores

Peer ratings are 1 to 10, submitted by players who attended, inside a 5-day window (`src/app/actions/ratings.ts:19-31`, window from `Activity.ratingWindowHours`, default 120, `prisma/schema.prisma:555`). They blend with a seed rating and feed the balancer (`src/lib/player-rating.ts:25-46`).

Ratings are stored on `User`, not on membership, and the aggregation query has no org filter (`prisma/schema.prisma:81-82`, `src/lib/team-generation.ts:88-92`). **A player in two clubs carries one rating into both.** Kemal is already in Sutton FC, so this is not hypothetical.

MoM is **voted, always**. There is no `Match.momWinnerId` column anywhere in the schema (`prisma/schema.prisma:640-708`), and the winner is derived by counting `MoMVote` rows each time (`src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:1904-1930`, tie handling in `src/lib/mom-announcement.ts:42-54`). With zero votes the announcement is skipped rather than invented (`src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:1931`). There is no product surface that lets an admin declare a winner; the only synthetic votes ever written came from an operator backfill script.

Scores are `redScore` and `yellowScore` (`prisma/schema.prisma:648-649`) and Elo only moves when teams were generated, which is why the "what was the score?" prompt is itself gated on team balancing being on (`src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:620-627`).

### 2.6 Payments

Per-match, per-player, in pounds, on the collector's own Stripe Express account via direct charges so MatchTime never holds the money (`src/lib/stripe.ts:12-15`, session created on the connected account at `:169`). Three methods, independently toggleable: pay by bank, card, and **pay the collector directly**, which is the cash or bank-transfer path where the collector confirms receipt (`prisma/schema.prisma:291-298`, confirm action `src/app/actions/payments.ts:320-331`, collector's roster page `src/app/collect/[matchId]/page.tsx`). The collector is `Organisation.paymentHolderId` and is excluded from the chase in five separate places (`prisma/schema.prisma:193`, e.g. `src/lib/payment-flow.ts:56`, `src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:1613`).

Two timing facts matter here:

- The fee is asked for **after the whistle**. The collector gets a DM at kick-off plus match duration asking how much per player (`src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:1551-1558`), confirms the amount, and only then are pay links released to CONFIRMED players (`src/lib/payment-flow.ts:34-88`, release gate `paymentLinksReleasedAt` at `prisma/schema.prisma:683`).
- Chasing runs daily at 18:00 for up to 10 days (`src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:1604-1607`).

`Activity.feePerPlayer` exists in the schema (`prisma/schema.prisma:562`) but **no application code reads it**. The fee is asked fresh every week.

There is exactly one `createCheckoutSession` call site in the codebase, and it can only be reached through a context that requires a Match with a fee and an Attendance row for the payer (`src/app/actions/payments.ts:188-234`). There is no subscription mode, no Stripe products, no invoices, and no refund path at all (the webhook ignores refund events, `src/app/api/stripe/webhook/route.ts:63`).

### 2.7 The recurring fixture model

One `Activity` is one weekday, one wall-clock time and one venue: `dayOfWeek Int`, `time String`, `venue String` (`prisma/schema.prisma:547-549`). The daily cron rolls it forward exactly one week at a time and creates one match (`src/app/api/cron/generate-matches/route.ts:69-78`, creation at `:123-131`).

`Match` has **no venue, no meet time, no opponent, no home or away field** (`prisma/schema.prisma:640-708`). Venue is always read through `activity.venue`.

`BlockBooking` pre-creates a whole season up front, which sounds promising, but it steps strictly `+7` days at a fixed weekday, time and venue (`src/lib/block-booking.ts:155`, spec vocabulary at `:58-72`). It expresses a season of identical fixtures, not a league schedule.

And the identity the system uses for "is this the same fixture" is `{orgId, venue, dayOfWeek}` plus kick-off proximity within 90 minutes (`src/lib/match-slot.ts:42-46`, tolerance at `:33`). Two league fixtures at the same ground on consecutive Saturdays would be indistinguishable to this code. That predicate is load-bearing for cron dedupe, for the format-switch ghost-match defence and for the posting-suppression gate, so it is the one place not to go poking for this customer.

### 2.8 Sport presets

`football-11aside` exists, with `playersPerTeam: 11`, positions GK, DEF, MID, FWD, a `positionComposition` of `{GK:1, DEF:4, MID:4, FWD:2}`, and MoM labelled "Man of the Match" (`src/lib/sport-presets.ts:38-46`). So the format is already there. `maxPlayers` for a match created on it is 22.

### 2.9 Per-org feature flags

Every module can be switched off independently (`prisma/schema.prisma:253-259`, resolved by `src/lib/org-features.ts:29-59`, enforced by a single filter over scheduler instructions at `src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:596-636`): `featureAttendance`, `featureBench`, `featureTeamBalancing`, `featureMomVoting`, `featurePlayerRating`, `featureReminders`, `featureStatsQa`, plus `paymentTrackingEnabled` and `paymentCollectionEnabled`. This is what makes a day-one configuration for a league team possible without any code.

## 3. The gaps, ranked

Ranked by how much of the managers' actual pain they block, not by size. "Agent-hours" means one Opus subagent working red, green, refactor, including unit and e2e tests and the main-thread review, on top of this codebase.

### G1. Availability is not selection. **Blocking. Real work: 10 to 14 agent-hours.**

Evidence for the need: L169, L193, L201. Evidence for the gap: `src/lib/attendance.ts:218-220` confirms whoever speaks first, `src/lib/squad-announce.ts:41-51` posts "squad locked" the moment the cap is hit, and bench offers then start backfilling a squad the managers have not picked.

If Walcountians ran with `maxPlayers` at 15, the first 15 to answer would be the squad, the bot would announce it as final, and the managers would have to undo it by hand. That is worse than the poll.

What it takes: an availability mode where every IN is recorded without occupying a slot, the "squad full" announcement and the bench-offer machinery stay quiet, and a manager publishes a selected squad in one action that then becomes what the group sees. The pieces already exist separately: squad surgery (`src/app/actions/players.ts:718`, `:834`, `:882`), a publish step (`src/app/actions/teams.ts:282`), and an append-only transition log. What is new is the state, one nullable per-match "selection published at" plus a capacity bypass, and the suppression rules that hang off it.

A cheaper 80% version needs no code at all: run them on the existing `football-11aside` preset so `maxPlayers` is 22, which a 20-strong availability list can never fill, so nothing is ever announced as locked and nobody is ever benched. The list is then just a list. The wrinkle is honest and should be said out loud: the chase copy would read "need 2 more" against 22 rather than against 15. See §4a.

### G2. No fixture list. **Blocking for the announcement, cheap to half-fix. 6 to 8 agent-hours for the useful half, 20 to 30 to do it properly.**

Evidence: L145 carries opponent, date, meet time, kick-off and venue in one line, and the venue changes every week (compare L129).

Evidence for the gap: venue lives on `Activity`, not `Match` (`prisma/schema.prisma:549`), the cron only ever rolls one weekday forward (`src/app/api/cron/generate-matches/route.ts:69-78`), there is no admin screen to edit a match's date or venue, and `BlockBooking` is weekly-identical by construction (`src/lib/block-booking.ts:155`).

The useful half: Walcountians genuinely do play every Saturday at the same kick-off time, so the recurring slot is right. What is missing is per-match **display**. Add three nullable columns on `Match` (opponent, venue override, home or away), one admin field, and thread them through four copy builders (announce, the 17:00 roster, the pre-kickoff post, the matchday reminder). That is additive and touches nothing structural.

The proper version means per-match venue as a real entity and a fixture-list import, which drags in `RecurringFixtureKey` (`src/lib/match-slot.ts:42-46`) and everything keyed off it. **Do not do this for one customer.**

### G3. Man of the Match is declared, not voted. **Small: 3 to 4 agent-hours.**

Evidence: the manager announces it in his match report (L143). Evidence for the gap: there is no column to record a declared winner (`prisma/schema.prisma:640-708`) and the winner is always recounted from votes (`src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:1904-1930`).

Two options. Give them the existing poll and change their ritual, which the group might actually enjoy, or add one nullable `Match.momWinnerUserId`, an admin intent, and precedence over the tally in `src/lib/mom.ts` and `src/lib/mom-announcement.ts`. The second is small, benefits every org, and removes the need for backfill scripts. Do the second and offer the first.

### G4. No meet time. **Small: 3 to 4 agent-hours.**

Evidence: stated every single week, L89, L105, and inside the poll title L145. Evidence for the gap: `Match.date` is the only time on a match (`prisma/schema.prisma:644`), everything else is derived from it, and an exhaustive search for a meet or arrival concept found nothing.

One nullable offset on `Activity` (meet minutes before kick-off) plus an optional per-match override, printed in the same four places as G2. Trivially useful and it costs almost nothing. It also helps any kickabout that has a changing-room time.

### G5. Selection needs a commitment table, and nobody has one. **4 to 6 agent-hours. The highest-value thing we could hand these two.**

Evidence: "the squad will be picked on the basis of previous availability and commitment" (L193), "Home games are of course popular" and away games are worse (L191, L201). They have stated their selection criterion and have no way to compute it.

MatchTime already stores every attendance transition with its cause in an append-only table (`prisma/schema.prisma:1166`, rationale `:1118-1164`), and has player stats and history reads. What is missing is the view: per player, availability rate over the last N fixtures, games played, how fast they answer, and (once G2 lands) the home versus away split they already suspect. This is mostly a read over data we would already be collecting, plus one chat answer behind `featureStatsQa`.

### G6. A matchday squad of 15 with 11 starting and 4 subs. **8 to 12 agent-hours.**

Evidence: a 15-man squad with a starting XI (L58), and minutes now owed against the fee (L65). Evidence for the gap: two teams only (`prisma/schema.prisma:24-27`), the balancer needs `perTeam * 2` and throws in a 15-man squad (`src/lib/team-balancer.ts:40-42`), surplus is discarded (`:58`, `:97`), and `BENCH` means the waiting list, not the dugout (`src/lib/attendance.ts:23`, `:218-220`).

There is a usable hack on day one: override the org's team labels to "Starting XI" and "Subs" (`Organisation.teamLabels`, `prisma/schema.prisma:217`, resolved at `src/lib/team-labels.ts:52-67`), then place 11 and 4 by hand on the teams screen using `addToTeam` and `removeFromTeam` (`src/app/actions/teams.ts:196`, `:214`) and publish. The automatic balancer will refuse to run, which is correct. Say that plainly rather than dressing it up.

Minutes tracking, which is what L65 actually promises the players, is a separate feature and is not in scope.

### G7. The money is collected before the game. MatchTime asks after. **5 to 6 agent-hours.**

Evidence: "Please transfer *BEFORE* the game so no headache" (L75), repeated the next morning with the reason (L103).

Evidence for the gap: the fee-ask DM fires at kick-off plus match duration (`src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:1551-1558`) and pay links are gated on the collector confirming it afterwards (`src/lib/payment-flow.ts:34-88`).

The rails themselves fit well. Their fee is a fixed £16 and their preferred method is bank transfer to the collector, which is exactly the "pay the collector directly" method with confirmation on `/collect/[matchId]`. What is needed is a fixed fee known up front and links released at announcement time rather than at the whistle. `Activity.feePerPlayer` already exists in the schema and is dead code (`prisma/schema.prisma:562`), so part of this is wiring something that was already designed.

### G8. Their poll is invisible to us. **6 to 8 agent-hours, and it should be built with G1 or not at all.**

Evidence: L144 to L150, the poll is the ritual. Evidence for the gap: the vote handler only recognises polls it posted (`src/app/api/whatsapp/poll-vote/route.ts:38-47`).

The bot can already post polls (the payment poll, `src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:1520-1524`). Making it post the availability poll and map votes onto availability preserves a habit that works, instead of asking 47 people to learn a new one. But without G1 the votes have nowhere useful to land.

### G9. No opponent, so no result. **Defer.**

Evidence: "3-1 to them" (L132). Evidence for the gap: scores are red against yellow (`prisma/schema.prisma:648-649`), and the score prompt is switched off entirely when team balancing is off (`src/lib/bot-scheduler.ts:620-627`), which is the configuration a league team wants.

A result, a table position and a form guide are all real league-team features and all genuinely new. There is a two-hour version (record a free-text result and echo it back) that is not worth doing on its own.

### G10. Kit money, and any one-off charge. **Decline for now: 12 to 16 agent-hours and it earns nothing.**

Evidence: £50 kit, once a season (L25, L84). Evidence for the gap: charging is structurally bound to a Match and an Attendance row, with one checkout call site (`src/app/actions/payments.ts:188-234`), no subscription mode, and no refund path.

They already handle it by bank transfer, once, in September. Building a second chargeable entity for that is the wrong trade.

### G11. Registration, eligibility and bans. **Decline.**

Evidence: L43, L45, L153, L154, L181. This is league administration, it lives on the county FA system, and it is not a WhatsApp problem.

### G12. Cross-org ratings. **A caution, not a gap. 2 to 3 agent-hours if it ever bites.**

If Walcountians turn peer ratings on, those ratings pool with any other MatchTime club the same player is in, because the rating lives on `User` and the query has no org filter (`prisma/schema.prisma:81-82`, `src/lib/team-generation.ts:88-92`). Kemal is in Sutton FC, so this is live from day one. The right answer for this customer is to leave ratings off, which sidesteps it.

## 4. What to offer

### (a) Works today, no code

Configuration only, on the existing `football-11aside` preset.

| Turn on | Why |
|---|---|
| `featureAttendance` | In and out in the group with no tag needed, one deterministic target match, and the numbered 17:00 list so everybody can find their own name |
| The whole chase ladder | 17:00 daily, matchday 08:00, 3 to 4 hours out, last chance. This replaces five manual chase posts a week |

## 5. Is this customer a good fit, or a distraction?

**The case against.** Everything MatchTime is distinctive at is wrong for them. The balancer, which is the cleverest thing in the codebase, cannot run. Elo cannot move. Peer ratings do not match their culture and leak across clubs. MoM is the wrong way round. The score columns cannot express their result. The fixture model assumes one venue on one weekday forever. Strip all that out and what is left is a chaser and a payment rail, which is the commoditised part. The venue strategy in `MDs/venue-commercialisation-strategy-2026-07-14.md` is built on retaining recurring block bookings, and a league side is not a block booking, so this customer does not feed that channel either. Chasing a second segment while the first still has one live club and a second in onboarding (`MDs/second-group-readiness-erdal-2026-09-16.md`) is a real cost.

**The case for.** The commoditised half is the half they are visibly bleeding time on: five chase posts in four days for 21 votes out of 47, plus two 50/50s tracked by hand. The at-risk warning already answers the exact question a player asked in the group. Three of the six changes in (b) are things the kickabout market wants anyway, and the most valuable one, the commitment table, is a read over data we would already be collecting. And Kemal is in the group every Saturday, which is design-partner access at zero acquisition cost, with two managers who have already written down their problem in their own words.

**Recommendation.** Run tier (a) as a free pilot on this one group for the rest of the season, with the misfitting modules off and the wrinkles stated in advance. Build item 1 (meet time) and item 2 (fixture line) before or during week one, because they are cheap and they are what makes the bot's posts look like they belong to this team rather than to a Tuesday kickabout. Hold items 3 to 6 until the managers have used it for three consecutive fixtures and asked for them. Do not describe "league teams" as a second market, and do not size the roadmap around them, until that has happened.

The thing that would change my mind either way is one measurable: does the number of chase posts the managers write per fixture fall. That is the whole value proposition for this customer, it is visible in the group without any instrumentation, and it is answerable in three weeks.

## 7. Notes for whoever picks this up

1. The transcript is not in this repo and must not be committed. It is one week of one real group's private messages.
2. Three unrelated things surfaced while reading the code and are worth their own tickets, independent of this customer:
   - `src/app/api/cron/close-ratings/route.ts:10-13` is a no-op that only authenticates and returns ok. Rating windows are checked per request and there is a 5-day scheduler backstop, so nothing is broken, but the route name promises something it does not do.
   - Three different rating formulas feed three different balancer entry points: the Bayesian blend in `src/lib/player-rating.ts:36` used by the live bot path, the older step function in `src/app/api/cron/generate-teams/route.ts:62-65`, and the only Elo-blended one in `src/app/actions/teams.ts:22-33`. The same match generated from the dashboard and from chat can produce different teams.
   - `prisma/schema.prisma:1081-1082` says `paidAt` can be toggled from the admin match page. There is no such toggle; the only web surface that marks a payment received is `/collect/[matchId]`.
3. `Activity.feePerPlayer` and `Activity.feeSplitTotal` (`prisma/schema.prisma:562`, `:565`) are read by no application code. Item 6 in §4b would be the first consumer, which is worth knowing before anyone assumes they are live.
