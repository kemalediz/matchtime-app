## Setting up privacy@matchtime.ai (for Kemal, copying the hometenant.ai setup)

Inbound through Google Workspace, outbound app mail through Resend, exactly as hometenant.ai
(from the HomeTenant session, 24 Oct). Only Kemal can do steps 1 to 4.

1. **Before switching anything:** in Namecheap > matchtime.ai > Email Forwarding, list every
   forward that exists today. Switching MX to Google ends them all; each must be recreated as a
   Workspace alias.
2. **Google Workspace Admin:** add matchtime.ai as a SECONDARY domain (not a user alias
   domain), verify it (Google gives a `google-site-verification=` TXT record), then add
   `privacy@matchtime.ai` (and any recreated forwards) as user aliases on kemal.ediz@cressoft.io.
   There is no catch-all: an address bounces until its alias exists.
3. **Namecheap DNS:** Mail Settings to "Custom MX", then:
   - MX `@` -> `smtp.google.com` (priority 1), and delete the eforward MX records
   - TXT `@` -> `v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all` (replacing the registrar SPF; add
     `include:amazonses.com` only if Resend sends from the root)
   - TXT `@` -> the Google verification value
   - TXT `_dmarc` -> `v=DMARC1; p=none;` (monitoring)
   - Resend records (step 4): MX `send` -> `feedback-smtp.eu-west-1.amazonses.com` (10),
     TXT `send` -> `v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all`, TXT `resend._domainkey` -> Resend's
     DKIM key
   - Gotcha: Namecheap Email Forwarding cannot coexist with the `send` MX, which is why inbound
     moves to Google.
4. **Resend dashboard (org cressoft, eu-west-1):** add and verify matchtime.ai, only if
   MatchTime should send app mail from it. Not needed for privacy@ to receive.
5. **To reply as privacy@ from Gmail** (still pending on hometenant.ai too): turn on Google
   DKIM (Admin > Apps > Gmail > Authenticate email, which adds `google._domainkey`), then
   Gmail > Settings > "Send mail as" for the alias.
6. **Then tell me:** I switch the one config value on the privacy page to privacy@matchtime.ai
   and check with dig that the records are live.
